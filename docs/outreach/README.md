# Shop outreach

Templates for contacting retailers. Two situations come up:

1. **The shop blocks our crawler.** We stop immediately and ask — we never work around a
   block. See `blocked-shop.md`.
2. **The shop is crawlable but a feed would be better.** A product feed is more accurate
   for them and cheaper for us, so it is always worth asking. See `feed-request.md`.

Fill in the contact details before sending; nothing here is sent automatically.

## What to offer

The pitch is referral traffic, not a favour. tike sends visitors who already know their
size and are ready to buy, and we can show per-shop click counts from the `click` table.
Lead with that, keep it short, and make the opt-out explicit — a shop that knows it can
leave at any time is much more likely to say yes.
